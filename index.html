What is Dungeons and Dragons?

Dungeons and Dragons is a type of game called a TTRPG (Tabletop Role-Playing Game). it is a very cheap game to start playing as all it requires is a set of dice, a peice of paper, and a pencil.

the average group size is 4-7 people with one of the people being the Dungeon Master or DM. this is not a required range, it is just recomended for the most fun.
